# Attribution And Forgery Policy

Attribution status must distinguish what a work claims about itself from what reviewed scholarship
or tradition says about it.

## Required Fields

- Claimed authorship.
- Scholarly attribution.
- Attribution status.
- Evidence basis.
- Review status.

## Attribution Categories

- `claimed_author_known`
- `anonymous`
- `traditional_attribution`
- `pseudepigraphal`
- `disputed_attribution`
- `false_attribution`
- `known_forgery`
- `modern_hoax`
- `unknown_unreviewed`

## Policy

Forgery or fake labels require evidence and review.

Do not over-label uncertain ancient pseudepigrapha as modern fraud without review. Ancient
pseudepigraphy, disputed attribution, false attribution, known forgery, and modern hoax are separate
categories.

Known fakes and confirmed forgeries must be quarantined. They may be preserved only for
identification, refutation, comparison, and contamination defense.

## Review Requirements

Before a work can leave quarantine:

1. claimed authorship is recorded;
2. scholarly attribution is recorded;
3. evidence basis is cited;
4. source status is reviewed;
5. trust level is assigned;
6. allowed and forbidden uses are explicit.
